\begin{notesbox}{Spiral Review --- A Summer Coding Course}
\normalsize
Last summer $200$ students took a tough coding course. The table shows who used the study app
and who went to the review session.

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
\begin{tabular}{l c c c}
\toprule
 & \textbf{Went to review} & \textbf{No review} & \textbf{Total} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Used the app} & $84$ & $36$ & $120$ \\
\textbf{No app} & $16$ & $64$ & $80$ \\
\textbf{Total} & $100$ & $100$ & $200$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=20pt, topsep=8pt]

  \item Read the table. How many students\ldots

        \vspace{4pt}
        \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{2.2}
        \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{} X p{2.6cm} @{}}
        used the app \emph{and} went to review? & \blank{2.2cm} \\
        used the app in all? & \blank{2.2cm} \\
        went to review in all? & \blank{2.2cm} \\
        \end{tabularx}

  \item Percent of a part. Show the division, then give the percent.

        \vspace{4pt}
        (a) What percent of the $120$ app users went to review?
        \begin{work}
          84 \div 120 &= 0.70 = 70\%
        \end{work}

        \vspace{10pt}
        (b) What percent of the $100$ students who went to review used the app?
        \begin{work}
          84 \div 100 &= 0.84 = 84\%
        \end{work}

  \item Compare the decimals. Write $<$, $>$, or $=$ in each blank.
        \wordbank{$<$ \;\textbullet\; $>$ \;\textbullet\; $=$}

        \vspace{2pt}
        \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{2.0}
        \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{} Y Y Y @{}}
        $0.70$ \; \blank{1.2cm} \; $0.50$ &
        $0.60$ \; \blank{1.2cm} \; $0.6$ &
        $0.41$ \; \blank{1.2cm} \; $0.8$ \\
        \end{tabularx}

        \vspace{6pt}
        How do you know which of $0.41$ and $0.8$ is greater?
        \par\writeline

\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}
