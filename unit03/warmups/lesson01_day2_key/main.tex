\begin{notesbox}{Spiral Review --- Two Bar Graphs, One Survey}
\normalsize
Oakdale High asked $80$ juniors which lunch line they use. Both graphs show the same answers.

\vspace{2pt}
{\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm, y=0.05cm]
  % frequency panel
  \foreach \v in {10,20,30,40} { \draw[linegray] (0,\v) -- (5.6,\v); }

  \draw[thick] (0,0) -- (0,43);

    \node[above, font=\scriptsize\bfseries] at ({0.75+\i*1.35},\n) {\n};
    \node[below, font=\scriptsize] at ({0.75+\i*1.35},0) {\lab};
  }
  \node[rotate=90, font=\scriptsize\itshape] at (-0.8,21) {juniors};
  % relative frequency panel (same bars; 10% of 80 = 8 juniors)
  \begin{scope}[xshift=7.6cm]

  \draw[thick] (0,0) -- (0,43);

    \node[below, font=\scriptsize] at ({0.75+\i*1.35},0) {\lab};
  }
  \node[rotate=90, font=\scriptsize\itshape] at (-0.95,21) {percent};
  \end{scope}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=8pt, topsep=6pt]

  \item Name each graph in the boxes. \par Sandwich line: \ans{20} juniors. \quad Pizza minus Salad: \ans{20} juniors.

  \item Relative frequency of Salad as a fraction and a percent: \ans{12/80 = 15\%}
        \par\vspace{3pt}What does it mean in context?\par\ansline{15\% of the 80 surveyed juniors use the Salad line.}

  \item The right graph shows $20\%$ for Hot meal. How many juniors is that? \ans{16 juniors}

\end{enumerate}
