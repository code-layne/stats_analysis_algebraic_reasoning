\begin{scenariobox}[Millbrook Public Library --- Which Program Next?]{cerulean}
\small
Millbrook has $1{,}200$ card holders and one grant to spend on a new program.
The director runs the survey properly: she draws a \textbf{simple random sample
of $80$} card holders from the membership list and reaches every one of them by
phone. Each is read the same item and picks one of four programs.

\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{The results:} evening book club $28$, teen coding lab $24$, Saturday
story hour $20$, job-search help $8$.

\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{Meanwhile}, a volunteer leaves a sign-up clipboard on the front desk for
a week. \textbf{$50$} people write something down: book club $10$, coding lab
$6$, story hour $30$, job-search help $4$.
\end{scenariobox}

\vspace{0.08in}

        \textbf{(a)} What percent of those $50$ people chose story hour?
