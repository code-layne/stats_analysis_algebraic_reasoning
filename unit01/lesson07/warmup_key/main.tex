\begin{notesbox}{Spiral Review --- Two Studies, One Study Center}
\small
Riverbend High School has now studied its after-school Study Center twice.
\textbf{Last quarter she watched:} the counselor pulled $150$ student records
--- $60$ students had used the Study Center and $90$ had not --- and counted how
many passed every class. \textbf{$39$} of the users did, and \textbf{$72$} of
the non-users did. \textbf{This quarter she assigned:} $120$ volunteers, split
by a random number generator into two groups of $60$, and $75\%$ of the treated
group against $55\%$ of the control group passed every class.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=8pt, topsep=4pt]

  \item In last quarter's records, what \textbf{percent} of the $60$ Study
        Center users passed every class?

        \begin{work}
          \dfrac{39}{60} &= 0.65 \\
                         &= 65\%
        \end{work}

  \item In last quarter's records, what \textbf{percent} of the $90$ non-users
        passed every class?

        \begin{work}
          \dfrac{72}{90} &= 0.80 \\
                         &= 80\%
        \end{work}

  \item Compare the two studies.

        \textbf{(a)} In last quarter's records, how many \textbf{percentage
        points} higher is the group that did \emph{not} use the Study Center?

        \begin{work}
          80 - 65 &= 15 \text{ percentage points}
        \end{work}

        \textbf{(b)} How many percentage points is this quarter's gap, and which
        of the two studies had two groups of the \textbf{same size}?

        \ans{$75 - 55 = 20$} \qquad \ans{this quarter's --- $60$ and $60$}

\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}
