% ── 1. THE FOUR STAGES ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}[t, plain]
  \ceruleanheader{1. The data cycle --- four stages, in order}
  \sectionlabel{and then it starts over}

  \vspace{0.2cm}
  \begin{center}
    \small
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
    \begin{tabular}{@{} c l l @{}}
    \hline
    \textbf{Stage} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{What it hands to the next stage} \\
    \hline
    1 & Formulate questions      & A question naming a group and a measurement \\
    2 & Collect or acquire data  & The raw values, one per individual \\
    3 & Organize and represent   & A table that makes the pattern visible \\
    4 & Analyze and communicate  & A conclusion in context --- and a new question \\
    \hline
    \end{tabular}
  \end{center}

  \vspace{0.5cm}
  \begin{center}
    {\color{cerulean}\bfseries Each stage can only use what the stage before it
    handed over.}
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

% ── 2. STAGE 1: THE QUESTION ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}[t, plain]
  \ceruleanheader{2. Stage 1 --- a question data can answer}
  \sectionlabel{three tests}

  \vspace{0.3cm}
  \begin{center}
    \begin{minipage}{0.8\textwidth}
      \begin{enumerate}
        \setlength{\itemsep}{6pt}
        \item Do the answers \textbf{vary} from one individual to the next?
        \item Does it name the \textbf{group} it is about?
        \item Does it say what will be \textbf{measured} or recorded?
      \end{enumerate}
    \end{minipage}
  \end{center}

  \vspace{0.4cm}
  \begin{columns}[t]
    \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
      \begin{block}{Not ready}

        ``Do people like biking?''\\[3pt]
        ``Is the breakfast cart good?''
      \end{block}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
      \begin{block}{Ready}
        ``How do \textbf{Riverbend students} usually \textbf{get to school}?''
        \\[3pt]
        A repair keeps the \textbf{original subject}.
      \end{block}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

% ── 3. THE QUESTION DECIDES THE TYPE ─────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}[t, plain]
  \ceruleanheader{3. The question decides the type of data}
  \sectionlabel[redacc]{before any data arrive}

  \vspace{0.3cm}
  \begin{columns}[t]
    \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
      \begin{block}{``How do students get to school?''}
        Record: the \textbf{way} each student travels.\\[4pt]
        {\color{cerulean}\bfseries Categorical} ---
        summarize with counts and percents.
      \end{block}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
      \begin{block}{``How many minutes is the trip?''}
        Record: the \textbf{minutes} each trip takes.\\[4pt]
        {\color{cerulean}\bfseries Quantitative} ---
        summarize with a mean.
      \end{block}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}

  \vspace{0.5cm}
  \begin{center}
    \begin{minipage}{0.85\textwidth}
      If you cannot finish ``for each student I will record
      \underline{\hspace{2.5cm}},'' the question is not finished either.
    \end{minipage}
  \end{center}

  \vspace{0.3cm}
  \begin{center}
    {\color{redacc}\bfseries Same 30 students, two questions, two types. The
    \emph{question} decided it.}
  \end{center}
\end{frame}

% ── 6. WHERE A STUDY BREAKS DOWN ─────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{frame}[t, plain]
  \ceruleanheader{6. Where a study breaks down}
  \sectionlabel[redacc]{where did the problem ENTER?}

  \vspace{0.2cm}
  \begin{center}
    \small
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
    \begin{tabular}{@{} p{8.6cm} c @{}}
    \hline
    \textbf{What went into the report} & \textbf{Stage} \\
    \hline
    Could not decide what to write down for each student. & 1 \\
    Only students locking bikes at the rack were asked. & 2 \\
    All 60 answers written out, never counted by group. & 3 \\

  \end{center}

  \vspace{0.5cm}
  \begin{center}

  \end{center}

  \vspace{0.3cm}
  \begin{center}
    The unanswered question sends the council back to stage 1. That is not
    failure --- that is how studies work.
  \end{center}
