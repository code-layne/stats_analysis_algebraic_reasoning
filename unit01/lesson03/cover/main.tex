\begin{remindbox}
\small
In a \textbf{probability sample}, chance chooses --- not the researcher, and not
who happens to be standing there. \textbf{Simple random}: number the frame and
draw. \textbf{Stratified}: some from every group, sized in proportion.
\textbf{Systematic}: a random start, then every $k$th one, where
$k = N \div n$. \textbf{Cluster}: everyone from a few randomly chosen groups.
The two that get mixed up are the middle pair --- \emph{strata are alike inside;
clusters are supposed to be a small mix of the whole}.
\end{remindbox}
