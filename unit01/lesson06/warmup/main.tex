\begin{notesbox}{Spiral Review --- Riverbend High School}
\small
Riverbend High School asked for volunteers to test its after-school Study
Center. \textbf{$120$ students volunteered.} A random number generator split
them into \textbf{two groups of $60$}: one group was told to attend twice a
week, the other group was told not to attend at all. At the end of the quarter
the counselor counted how many students in each group passed every class.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=8pt, topsep=4pt]

  \item $45$ of the $60$ students who attended passed every class. What
        \textbf{percent} of that group is it?

        \begin{work}
          \dfrac{45}{60} &= 0.75 \\
                         &= 75\%
        \end{work}

  \item $33$ of the $60$ students who did not attend passed every class. What
        \textbf{percent} of that group is it?

        \begin{work}
          \dfrac{33}{60} &= 0.55 \\
                         &= 55\%
        \end{work}

  \item Compare the two groups.

        \textbf{(a)} How many \textbf{percentage points} higher is the group
        that attended?

        \begin{work}
          75 - 55 &= 20 \text{ percentage points}
        \end{work}

        \textbf{(b)} How many of the $120$ volunteers passed every class in
        total, and what percent of all $120$ is that?

        \blank{2.4cm} \qquad \blank{2.4cm}

\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}
