\parthead{Part C --- Short Computations \hfill 4 questions, 10 points each}

\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em, itemsep=10pt, topsep=2pt, start=16]

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- C1 (1.2)
\item \textbf{Pine Valley Aquatic Center} has $1{,}200$ members. The director asks
$60$ members chosen at random, and $21$ of them take a swim class.

\vspace{2pt}
(a) What percent of the sample takes a swim class? \hfill\smash{\gradedblank[id=q16a, type=short, points=5, answer={35}, check=numeric, display={35\%}]{1.6cm}{10pt}}

\begin{work}
  \dfrac{21}{60} &= 0.35 = 35\%
\end{work}

(b) About how many of all $1{,}200$ members take a swim class? \hfill\smash{\gradedblank[id=q16b, type=short, points=5, answer={420}, check=numeric]{1.6cm}{10pt}}

\begin{work}
  0.35 \times 1200 &= 420 \text{ members}
\end{work}

and $25$ of the usual group passed a quiz.

\vspace{2pt}
(a) Find the percent who passed in each group. \hfill\smash{\gradedblank[id=q19a1, type=short, points=3, answer={70}, check=numeric, display={70\%}]{1.6cm}{10pt}}\quad\smash{\gradedblank[id=q19a2, type=short, points=3, answer={50}, check=numeric, display={50\%}]{1.6cm}{10pt}}

  \text{Game: } \dfrac{35}{50} &= 0.70 = 70\% \\
  \text{Usual: } \dfrac{25}{50} &= 0.50 = 50\%

\item A random sample of Hillcrest students found that students who \textbf{play a

assigned --- students chose for themselves. The principal says, \emph{``Playing an
instrument causes higher math grades.''}

\vspace{3pt}
Is the principal's conclusion justified? Explain your reasoning, and name one
\textbf{other variable} that could explain the result.

\par\gradedwriteline[{\gradedblank[id=q20, type=rubric, points=20, rubric=../rubrics/saar-u01-actual-test-q20.yaml, display={No. Nobody was assigned --- students chose --- so this is an observational study. Only an experiment that randomly assigns instruments could show it causes higher grades.}]{\linewidth}{99.5pt}}]
