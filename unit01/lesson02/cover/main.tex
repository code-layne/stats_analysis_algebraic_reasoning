\begin{remindbox}
\small
A \textbf{parameter} describes the whole \textbf{p}opulation and is almost always
unknown; a \textbf{statistic} describes the \textbf{s}ample you actually collected
and is the number you can compute. Two honest samples from the same population
will give two different statistics --- that is \textbf{sampling variability}, not
a mistake. A conclusion may describe only the population your sample came from.
\end{remindbox}
