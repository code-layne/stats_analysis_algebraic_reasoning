  \item \ldots{} recognize a variable written with digits that is really a label,

\begin{remindbox}
\small
A \textbf{quantitative} variable is a measured amount with units --- you may
average it. A \textbf{categorical} variable is a label --- you may only count it
and turn the counts into percents. When a variable is written with digits, do not
guess: ask whether \emph{averaging these would mean anything}.
\end{remindbox}
