\begin{remindbox}
\small
A number by itself answers nothing. ``$18$'' is not data --- ``the six students
surveyed had a mean commute of $18$ minutes'' is. Before you compute anything, ask
what \textbf{type} the variable is: a \textbf{quantitative} variable records an
amount and can be averaged; a \textbf{categorical} variable records a label and can
only be counted and turned into a percent. And the test for a column of digits is
never ``does it look like a number?'' --- it is \textbf{``does arithmetic on it mean
anything?''}
\end{remindbox}
