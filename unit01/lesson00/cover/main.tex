\begin{learningtargetbox}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item \ldots{} explain why a number tells you nothing until I know \emph{who} was
        measured, \emph{what} was measured, and in \emph{what units}.
  \item \ldots{} read a data table and say who each row is about and what each column
        records.
  \item \ldots{} decide whether a column can honestly be averaged or only counted, and
        justify my decision from the data.
  \item \ldots{} spot a column written with digits that still cannot be averaged, and
        explain how I know.
  \item \ldots{} tell the difference between a question one look-up answers and a question
        that takes data from many people, and rewrite one into the other.
\end{enumerate}
\end{learningtargetbox}
