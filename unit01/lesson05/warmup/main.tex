\begin{notesbox}{Spiral Review --- Harbor Point Community Pool}
\small
Harbor Point Community Pool drew a random sample of $200$ of its $1{,}500$
members from the full membership list, and every one of them answered. Of the
$200$, \textbf{$80$ swim in the morning} and \textbf{$120$ swim in the evening}.
Each was asked whether they usually get at least $7$ hours of sleep.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=8pt, topsep=4pt]

  \item $56$ of the $80$ morning swimmers said yes. What \textbf{percent} of the
        morning swimmers is that?

        \begin{work}
          \dfrac{56}{80} &= 0.70 \\
                         &= 70\%
        \end{work}

  \item $54$ of the $120$ evening swimmers said yes. What \textbf{percent} of the
        evening swimmers is that?

        \begin{work}
          \dfrac{54}{120} &= 0.45 \\
                          &= 45\%
        \end{work}

  \item Compare the two groups.

        \textbf{(a)} How many \textbf{percentage points} higher is the morning
        group?

        \begin{work}
          70 - 45 &= 25 \text{ percentage points}
        \end{work}

        \textbf{(b)} How many of the $200$ members in the sample said yes in
        total, and what percent of the sample is that?

        \blank{2.4cm} \qquad \blank{2.4cm}

\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}
