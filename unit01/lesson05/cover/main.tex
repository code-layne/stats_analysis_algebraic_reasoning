\begin{remindbox}
\small
\textbf{In an observational study you watch --- you do not assign.} The groups
already exist, because the people in them chose. That is why a perfectly
unbiased observational study can still fail to prove a claim: when two variables
move together they are \textbf{associated}, and an association has at least three
possible explanations --- the claim, the same arrow \emph{backwards}, or a
\textbf{lurking variable} outside the study that moves both. Report what you
saw. Never write \emph{causes}, \emph{makes}, or \emph{improves}.
\end{remindbox}
