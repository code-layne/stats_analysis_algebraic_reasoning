\begin{notesbox}{Spiral Review --- Back to Unit 1: Variables and Frequency Tables}
\normalsize
Ms.~Ortiz's first-period class at Fox Run High has $20$ students. Each one answered three
questions: how many hours they slept last night, how they get to school (bus, car, walk, or
bike), and how many minutes the trip takes.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=22pt, topsep=10pt]

  \item Classify each variable, and give its units if it has any.
        \wordbank{quantitative \;\textbullet\; categorical \;\textbullet\; hours \;\textbullet\; minutes \;\textbullet\; none}

        \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.8}
        \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{} X p{4.2cm} p{2.6cm} @{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Variable} & \textbf{Quantitative or categorical?} & \textbf{Units} \\
        \midrule
        Hours of sleep last night  & \ans{quantitative} & \ans{hours} \\
        How you get to school      & \ans{categorical} & \ans{none} \\
        Minutes to get to school   & \ans{quantitative} & \ans{minutes} \\
        \bottomrule
        \end{tabularx}

  \item Ms.~Ortiz grouped the trip times into a frequency table.

        \vspace{4pt}
        \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.6}
        \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{} l *{5}{Y} @{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Minutes}  & $0$--$9$ & $10$--$19$ & $20$--$29$ & $30$--$39$ & $40$--$49$ \\
        \midrule
        \textbf{Students} & $7$ & $7$ & $3$ & $2$ & $1$ \\
        \bottomrule
        \end{tabularx}

        \vspace{8pt}
        How many students in all? \ans{20}\par\vspace{4pt}
        How many take $20$ minutes or more? \ans{6}

        \vspace{8pt}
        What \textbf{percent} of the class takes less than $10$ minutes?

        \begin{work}
          \dfrac{7}{20} = 0.35 = 35\%
        \end{work}

  \item The longest trip in the class is $47$ minutes. Write one sentence that says what $47$
        means --- name the individual, the variable, and the units.
        \par\ansline{One student in the class takes 47 minutes to get to school.}

        \vspace{6pt}
        Is a $47$-minute trip typical or unusual for this class? \ans{unusual}

\end{enumerate}
\end{notesbox}
