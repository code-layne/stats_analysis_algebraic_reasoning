\begin{skillbox}[Independent Practice (10 min)]{redbox}
\small
\textbf{Silently, alone, no partners.} Students work the notes' closing practice box, items
1--TODO. Say the rule out loud when you release it --- this is the phase that drifts into
partner work if you do not. The gold \emph{Extension} item is for students who finish early,
not a separate assignment.

\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{The arc.} TODO: item 1 is the on-ramp --- every student must be writing within thirty
seconds; \textbf{which item is the crux} (the second instance of today's trap); and what the
closing interpret-in-context sentence has to name.

\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{Two-minute check.} By 2:00 every student should have TODO. If more than a third do not,
stop the room and re-model TODO from the We Do.

\textbf{Circulate with questions, not answers.} \emph{Item N:} ``TODO prompt.''

\begin{skillbox}[Debrief (7 min) --- what goes on the board]{frost}
Run the displayed practice work in this order. \textbf{Students correct their own answers in a
second color} as you go; that self-correction is the record you collect from.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.3em, nosep, itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Item N.} TODO.
  \item \textbf{Item N --- the crux.} TODO: put both displayed answers up and land the rule
        out loud, in one sentence.
  \item \textbf{Extension item N}, if anyone reached it --- otherwise pose it aloud. TODO.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{Check for understanding --- ask it cold, whole class.} TODO the question. Hands down,
thirty seconds of think time, then cold-call three students. Correct answer: TODO.
\emph{This replaces the exit ticket.}

\vspace{2pt}
\textbf{Formative read.} Sort the answers into three piles: \textbf{(a)} TODO correct and
justified; \textbf{(b)} TODO correct for the wrong reason --- the big, fixable middle;
\textbf{(c)} TODO missed it. Open the next lesson from pile (b).
\end{skillbox}
